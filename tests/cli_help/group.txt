Usage: pixelforge [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

  Deterministic conversion of images into 16-bit-style pixel art.

  Results go to stdout (JSON lines); progress (-v), warnings and errors go to
  stderr.

Commands:
  batch     Convert a directory's images with a shared palette.
  compare   Compare box, kopf and gerstner side by side.
  convert   Convert one image.
  edit      Open a pixelforge output in the editor.
  palettes  List the bundled palettes.

Options:
      --version     Show the version and exit.
      --debug       Print full tracebacks on errors (also: PIXELFORGE_DEBUG=1).
  -v, --verbose...  Progress on stderr: -v per stage, -vv every iteration.
  -q, --quiet       Only errors on stderr (no warnings or hints).
      --help        Show this message and exit.
